Information we collect

XLibrary 2.0 - Your Library is a reader and library manager. The app can be used without creating an account, but some optional features require account data so users can sync and restore their library data across devices.

If you choose to create or use an account, we may collect and process information needed for account access and app functionality, including:

  • Email address, username, and login credentials
  • Phone number, if you provide one during account setup or profile use
  • Account status and account identifiers
  • Saved library sources, synced library records, reading state, and sync preferences
  • App interactions related to library management, sync, backup, and restore features

XLibrary does not use the app to collect your contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, SMS, call logs, payment card data, government ID, or unrelated device files.

Third-party SDKs and external libraries

XLibrary uses Google Analytics/Firebase Analytics, Firebase services, Google AdMob, and related Google SDKs. These services may collect app activity, device information, advertising identifiers, crash logs, diagnostics, and ad-related signals according to Google's policies and user consent settings.

The app may allow users to add and manage external library sources. External library sources are controlled by third parties, so their privacy and security practices may differ from XLibrary. XLibrary does not own or control the content, privacy practices, or data handling of third-party library websites and sources.

How we use information

We use collected information only for appropriate app and service purposes, including:

  • Creating, signing in to, and managing user accounts
  • Providing cloud sync, backup, restore, and reading-state features
  • Helping users manage saved library sources and preferences
  • Improving app reliability, diagnosing crashes, and fixing bugs
  • Showing ads, measuring ad performance, and preventing ad abuse where Google AdMob is used
  • Protecting account security, preventing abuse, and complying with applicable legal obligations

XLibrary does not sell personal data.

Security, retention, and account deletion

XLibrary uses HTTPS for official server communication where available. External library sources may use their own security practices and are outside our direct control.

Account and sync data is retained only as long as needed to provide account-based features, maintain service security, prevent abuse, meet operational needs, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

You may request account deletion at any time. Verified and approved deletion requests are processed within 30 days. Account profile information, login or account identifiers, synced library data, saved library sources, reading or sync preferences, and other data directly associated with the account are deleted unless limited retention is required for security, abuse prevention, fraud investigation, legal compliance, or operational logs.

Limited retained records are normally deleted or anonymized within 90 days unless a longer retention period is required by law or an active security investigation.
