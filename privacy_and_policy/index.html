Privacy Policy

This privacy notice explains what XLibrary handles directly and what may be collected by third-party SDKs used in the app.

XLibrary Privacy Policy

1. XLibrary uses Google Analytics/Firebase Analytics SDK and Google AdMob SDK. These Google SDKs may collect app activity, device information, advertising identifiers, crash/diagnostic information, and ad-related signals according to Google's policies and user consent settings.

2. XLibrary itself does not sell personal data. XLibrary does not use the app to collect your contacts, location, camera, microphone, SMS, call logs, payment card data, government ID, or device files.

3. XLibrary does not scan or collect the contents of your all libraries and books, book pages, comic pages, episode images, or reading files from third-party library sources. Library and book content is loaded for reading on your device, and XLibrary may not know what every external source contains.

4. If you choose to create or use an account, only the information needed for account access, saved library list review, library sync, backup, restore, and related app features may be used, such as login credentials, account status, saved library source records, and reading/sync state. Email and phone information are optional where the app allows them. You may leave optional profile fields empty or use non-personal/random values if you do not want to provide real contact details.

5. Saved library source records may be stored only to support account sync, backup, restore, and library management features. XLibrary does not claim ownership of your library sources and does not use them to identify the contents of third-party pages.

6. XLibrary uses HTTPS for official server communication where available. External library sources are controlled by third parties, so their privacy and security practices may differ from XLibrary.

7. You may request account deletion at any time through XLibrary support. When an account deletion request is processed, account data associated with that account will be deleted unless limited retention is required for security, abuse prevention, legal, or operational reasons.

8. You can use the app without providing optional email or phone profile details where those fields are not required for the specific feature you are using.

9. For privacy, account deletion, copyright, or external library concerns, contact XLibrary support from the app's Settings page.

Continuing to use this app means you have read, understood, and agreed to this Privacy Policy.
