How to request account deletion

For account security and abuse prevention, XLibrary 2.0 - Your Library reviews account deletion requests manually before completion. If you want to delete your account, please contact the developer support email below and our team will verify the request before approving deletion on the server.

Please send your request to:

contact4shiba@gmail.com

Your request should be sent from the same email address that was used to create or access your XLibrary 2.0 - Your Library account. If we cannot verify ownership from that email alone, we may ask for additional account-verification details before processing the request.

Please include the following information in your email:

  • Email address used for the account
  • Phone number used for the account, if you provided one
  • Username
  • A short note saying that you want to delete your XLibrary 2.0 - Your Library account

After we receive your request, we will review it and may contact you for verification if needed. Verified and approved account deletion requests are processed within 30 days.

Data deleted after approval

When an account deletion request is verified and approved, XLibrary 2.0 - Your Library deletes data directly associated with the account, including:

  • Account profile information
  • Login or account identifiers, including email address and phone number if provided
  • Username and account metadata
  • Synced library data
  • Saved library sources
  • Reading or sync preferences associated with the account

Data retained and retention period

XLibrary 2.0 - Your Library, published by SHIBA SARAN, may retain limited records only when needed for security, abuse prevention, fraud investigation, legal compliance, or operational logs. These limited records are kept only as long as necessary and are normally deleted or anonymized within 90 days unless a longer retention period is required by law or an active security investigation.

Please do not include passwords, payment information, government ID, or other sensitive personal information in your email.
